6. Working Principle

When two coherent light waves from S₁ and S₂ overlap, their superposition produces alternate maxima and minima.

Constructive Interference

Path difference = nλ. The waves reinforce one another, producing a bright fringe.

Destructive Interference

Path difference = (n + ½)λ. The waves cancel one another, producing a dark fringe.

Ideal Intensity Equation

I(y) = Imax cos²(πdy / λD)

This model assumes two equally illuminated coherent slits and uses the small-angle approximation. It does not include the single-slit diffraction envelope.
